# Summarise

Your task it to summarize the content of the following URL: $ARGUMENTS

Focus on the main arguments, facts, or conclusions. Avoid copying text directly from the page.

Please present the summary in 3–5 bullet points, suitable for someone who needs a quick, accurate overview of the content."

Do not ask for confirmation to fetch the URL. Just fetch the content to summarize it directly without any additional questions or prompts.
